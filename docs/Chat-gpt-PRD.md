# PRD: AI-Powered Home Services Marketplace (Uber + AI for Remodeling & Lawn Care)

---

# 1. Product Overview

## Product Name

**(Working Name)**: Projexlight HomeAI Marketplace

## Vision

Create an AI-powered platform where homeowners can **upload videos/images of their home projects** and instantly receive:

- Structured scope of work
- Cost estimates
- Timeline
- Matched contractors
- Auto-generated bids

👉 Goal: Reduce **project uncertainty, time-to-start, and rework** in home services.

# 6. AI System Design

---

## 6.1 Input Processing Pipeline

**Inputs:**

- Video
- Images
- Text

**Steps:**

1. Frame extraction (video)
2. Object detection (rooms, lawn, fixtures)
3. Classification (type of job)
4. Feature extraction

---

## 6.2 Scope Generation Engine

Outputs:

- Task list
- Material list
- Labor hours
- Dependencies

---

## 6.3 Pricing Engine

Inputs:

- Historical jobs
- Location data
- Material costs

Outputs:

- Price range
- Confidence score

---

## 6.4 Matching Engine

Factors:

- Contractor skill match
- Past performance
- Availability
- Price competitiveness

---

## 6.5 Continuous Learning Loop

- Actual vs estimated cost
- Job success data
- Customer feedback
